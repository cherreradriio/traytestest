Magnets & stacking

Square-sided, open-face pockets let you pry magnets out for replacement. Use removable adhesive if needed and mark each magnet's polarity before gluing: mating faces (tray top/lid bottom, tray bottom/lid top) need opposite poles, and every pocket on a face should face the same way. Corner magnets grip best against lid lift but need thicker walls. The 6 × 2 mm default needs thicker walls, floor, and lid than the basic tray.

Use either or both: matching low feet under the tray and a raised rim on the lid help keep stacked trays aligned. Stacking feet replace the separate corner feet.

Plug lid: a shallow insert fits inside the tray opening. Increase clearance if your printer tends to run tight.

Mesh check

The tray is open at the top by design; the lid closes it when assembled. Start with the balanced 0.30 mm-per-side preset, then tune for your printer and material. Choose tray-only, lid-only, or separated views to inspect each part, then flip the view to inspect undersides. Stack alignment adds feet under the tray and a locating rim on the lid. Parts download separately in millimetres. Check clearances and mesh unions in your slicer, then test-fit before a full print.
